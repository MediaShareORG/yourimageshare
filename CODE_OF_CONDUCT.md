# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project - its issues, pull requests, and any other space where
the community interacts - to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, code review, and issue
  reports.
- Assume good faith; disagreements happen, but keep them focused on the
  work.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spamming issues/PRs.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages) and to any other setting where someone is representing the
project or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it via
[yourimageshare.com/contact](https://yourimageshare.com/contact). Reports
are handled privately and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull
requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful.
